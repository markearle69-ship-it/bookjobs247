Questions

Does my phone still ring?

Yes. You set forwarding for calls you don't answer, are busy for, or get when you have no signal. Pick up as normal when you can.

Do I need a new number or phone?

No. Customers keep calling your usual number. Your {{ settings.app_name }} number works behind the scenes.

Will callers know it's AI?

It sounds natural and answers as your business. If someone asks directly, it's honest that it's an automated assistant taking messages for you.

Does it book jobs or quote prices?

No, on purpose. It takes a complete message so you can price and book the job when you call back.

What if I go over {{ plan.included_minutes }} minutes?

Extra minutes are {{ plan.overage_label }} each, billed with your monthly payment. A typical message takes 1–2 minutes.

Can I cancel?

Any time, in the app. No contract, no cancellation fee.
