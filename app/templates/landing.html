How it works

1. Forward missed calls

Takes 30 seconds. Your phone still rings first, and only calls you don't pick up come to us. We give you one-tap set-up buttons.

2. Your assistant answers

A friendly, natural-sounding voice answers in your business's name and takes a proper message: who, where, what the job is and how urgent it is.

3. Call them back

You get a notification with the job details. Finish your job, open the app, and tap to call them back.

On or off with one tap

Want calls to go to voicemail instead? Flick the switch in the app. When the assistant is off, voicemails still come to the app with a transcript.
