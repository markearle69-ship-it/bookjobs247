  2. Test it

    Ask someone to ring your number (or use another phone) and don't answer. Your assistant should pick up. The call then appears in your Calls list.

  3. Add the app to your home screen

    iPhone: in Safari tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
    Android: in Chrome tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

    Open it from your home screen and turn on notifications so you know about every call.
